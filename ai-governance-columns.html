Who Actually Governs AI?

AI governance is not one person, one law, or one institution. It is the sum of dozens of actors — each with different knowledge, different interests, and very different kinds of power.

Every actor on this map influences and is influenced by actors in other layers. The connections run in every direction simultaneously. No layer operates in isolation — which is precisely why AI governance is so difficult to get right.
